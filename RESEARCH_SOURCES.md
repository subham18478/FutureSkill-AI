# FutureSkill AI --- Research Sources

## 1. World Economic Forum --- Future of Jobs Report 2025

https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/

Relevance: supports the focus on changing skills, AI and big data,
analytical thinking, technological literacy and reskilling.

## 2. International Labour Organization --- Generative AI and Jobs: A 2025 Update

https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-and-jobs-2025-update

Relevance: provides an updated task-level assessment of occupational
exposure to generative AI and notes that many jobs are likely to be
transformed rather than fully eliminated.

## 3. International Labour Organization --- Generative AI and Jobs: A Global Analysis

https://www.ilo.org/publications/generative-ai-jobs-global-analysis-potential-effects-job-quantity-and

Relevance: provides research and methodology for analysing occupational
exposure to generative AI.

## 4. SQLite --- Copyright / Public Domain

https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html

Relevance: documents the public-domain status of SQLite's core database.

## 5. Flask --- License

https://flask.palletsprojects.com/en/stable/license/

Relevance: documents Flask's BSD-3-Clause license.

## 6. scikit-learn Documentation

https://scikit-learn.org/stable/

Relevance: reference documentation if scikit-learn is used in the
project's model layer.

### How the research is used

These sources support the problem statement and the project's focus on
future skills, AI impact, skill gaps and reskilling. External research
should be clearly distinguished from results produced by the project's
own dataset and analysis.
